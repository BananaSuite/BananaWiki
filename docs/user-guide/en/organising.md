# Organising the wiki

What you can do here depends on your permissions; administrators can do all
of it.

Use **New page** in the sidebar to start a page. The editor has **Create page** or
**Save**, plus **Cancel**, at the top and bottom.

## Categories

Categories are the folders of the sidebar and can contain subcategories.

* **New category** in the sidebar creates one (optionally inside another).
* Drag pages and categories in the sidebar to reorder or nest them.
* A category's page (click its name) lets you rename it, switch
  **sequential navigation** on (previous/next links between its pages, in
  sidebar order) and delete it. When deleting, choose what happens to its
  pages: move them to another category, leave them without a category, or
  delete them. Subcategories move up one level.

Some people may only read or write in certain categories. If a category you
expect is missing, you probably do not have access to it.

## Moving, renaming and changing the address

From the page's **Page actions** menu:

* **Rename** changes the title.
* **Change address** changes `/page/<address>`; links to the old address in
  other pages are updated.
* **Move** puts the page into another category.
* **Make home page** makes it the wiki's front page.

## Hiding and deleting

* **Hide from navigation and search** keeps a page reachable by its link but
  removes it from the sidebar and search results for most readers. **Show in
  navigation and search** undoes it.
* **Delete** removes the page. If the wiki uses *deletion slowdown*, the page
  first waits 48 hours, during which an administrator can restore it.

## Protected and reserved pages

* A **protected** page is controlled by one editor; nobody else can change it.
  An administrator who needs it can request an unlock, which takes effect
  after 72 hours unless the controller releases it earlier.
* A **reserved** (checked-out) page is being worked on by one editor for a
  limited time; others cannot edit it meanwhile. Reserve a page from its
  menu or while saving; release it when you are done. **Reservations** in the
  Apps menu lists all reservations; each editor can hold a limited number and
  may ask for more.

## Proposing an edit

If you may read a page but not edit it, the wiki may offer **Propose an
edit**: change the text, explain why, and send it. A reviewer approves or
denies it; approved edits are saved under your name. **My contributions**
lists your proposals and their status. The number of waiting proposals per
person is limited.

## Difficulty tags

Pages can carry a level (beginner, easy, intermediate, expert, extra) or a
custom coloured label, useful for courses and tutorials. Editors set it from
the page.

## Scheduled changes

Administrators can schedule a page to be deleted, hidden or shown at a set
time. A page that is about to be deleted may show a countdown.
