# Your account

Open **Settings** at the top right. On phones, open it from the account menu.

## Profile

Your real name, a short bio, your birth date and a picture. If profiles are
enabled you can **publish** your profile so other members see it, and fill in
the profile fields your wiki defined. **My profile** shows it as others see
it.

## Customise

Open **Customise** in the top bar (or the **Customise** tab of Settings). These change
how the wiki looks for you only: light or dark theme, text size, line and
letter spacing, content and sidebar width, contrast, your own colours, a
background picture, a dyslexia-friendly font, reduced motion, and how
strongly bold text, links, headings and code stand out. **Reset display
preferences** returns to the site's defaults and removes your background
picture; your language and mascot choices stay as they are.

**BM mode** is the easy mode: simpler screens (a shorter editor toolbar, fewer
menus) and fewer features to find your way through. When your wiki allows
it, you switch it on or off for your own account here; otherwise the page
says that your administrators decide. When the whole wiki uses BM mode,
everyone is in it.

In BM mode the sidebar shows the pages, Kanban boards and canvases only,
pages leave out the link to their history and their difficulty tag, and
Settings shows your profile, user name, password, language, email address
and **Customise**. The rest (sessions, two-step verification, your data,
account merges, deleting the account) is under **All settings** at the bottom
of the Settings page. Your administrators choose which of these
simplifications apply.

Some wikis show a mascot, a banana, in place of the logo in the top bar.
Clicking it makes it hop; the site name next to it leads to the home page. It
stays in the top bar for as long as your administrators keep it on. If you
found out how to put sunglasses on it, you can take them off here under
**Customise**.

## Language

Choose the interface language on the **Account** tab of Settings (before
you sign in, **Customise** has it too): English, Italian, German and any
language the administrators added, as long as they have not switched it off.
Pages themselves are not translated.

## Password and user name

Change your password in Settings. Doing so signs out your other sessions (the
current one stays) and stops your API tokens. A new password needs at least 8
characters, must not contain your user name or the part of your email address
before the @, and must not be one of the most common passwords. Changing your
user name leaves pages as they are: `@mentions` of your old name still lead to
your profile, and nobody else can take that name unless an administrator
releases it. You can change it three times a day.

## Sessions

**Sessions** lists where you are signed in (browser, address, last activity).
End a single session, or **Sign out everywhere** if you lost a device or
suspect someone knows your password. The wiki may be set to allow only one
session per account: signing in elsewhere then signs you out here.

## Your data

**Your data → Download my data** gives you a ZIP with your account, profile,
preferences, contributions and uploaded files.

## API tokens

If the wiki's REST API is on and you are allowed to use it, **API tokens**
creates tokens for scripts. A token can do at most what you can do, only
within the scopes you give it, and is shown once. Revoke tokens you no longer
use.

## Merging accounts

If you have two accounts, the account merge page in Settings
(`/settings/merge-request`) asks to merge one into the other. Both accounts
confirm with their passwords, then an administrator approves.

## Deleting your account

**Delete account** removes your account permanently. Pages you wrote stay;
their history no longer names you. Kanban boards you created pass to an
administrator, and your tickets and comments stay on their boards under the
board owner's name; each comment starts with a note saying you wrote it.

## If your account is blocked

A suspended account, or one waiting for approval, only sees a status page
explaining why (and until when, if the administrator chose to show it).
Contact an administrator of the wiki.
