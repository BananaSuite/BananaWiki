# Reading and finding things

## Signing in

Open the wiki and sign in with your user name and password. Tick **Keep me
signed in for 30 days** on your own device; otherwise you are
signed out when you close the browser (and after 7 days at the latest).
Depending on the wiki you can create an account yourself (**Create an account**; you
may need an invite code, and an administrator may have to approve you), or an
administrator creates it for you. Some wikis are in **public mode**: then you
can read without an account.

The first time you sign in you may be asked to change your password and see a
short introduction. You can replay the tour later from the account menu.

## Finding your way

* **The sidebar** lists the categories and their pages. Click a category to
  open or close it; long categories show *More pages…*. The field at the top
  filters page and category titles as you type; tick *Search in text too* to
  search the text as well.
* **Home** is the wiki's front page. **All pages** (`/navigation`) shows the
  whole tree.
* **Apps** at the bottom of the sidebar lists the tools your wiki has switched on: kanban
  boards, canvases, messages, groups, people, the leaderboard.
* The **theme** button switches between light and dark.

## Search

Press Enter in the sidebar field or open **Search** (`/search`). All words
must appear. You can also write:

| Write | Finds |
|---|---|
| `"exact phrase"` | the words in this order |
| `-word` | pages without the word |
| `title:budget` | the word in the title |
| `content:budget` | the word in the text |
| `category:handbook` | pages in categories with that name |
| `slug:setup` | the word in the page address |
| `type:page` or `type:category` | only pages or only categories |

The search page can also limit the search to titles, text or category names,
and sort by relevance, title or most recent edit. You only ever see pages you
are allowed to read.

## A page

At the top you see the title, the category path and who edited it last. Long
pages have a **Contents** list of their headings. Categories with
*sequential navigation* show previous/next links at the bottom, useful for
courses and tutorials.

The **Page actions** menu has, depending on your rights: **History**, **Print**,
export as PDF or Markdown, and editor actions. Under the page you may find
attachments to download, a quiz, and the audio player.

## Listening to a page

If the wiki has read aloud, a **Listen to this page** player sits under the
text. When the page has no audio yet, press **Generate audio** and wait a
moment (only a few pages can be queued at once). Choose a speed from 0.75× to
2× and download the audio if you like. The audio is renewed after the page is
edited.

## People

**People** (`/users`) lists the members. A profile shows what the person
chose to publish: name, bio, custom fields, badges and contributions.
