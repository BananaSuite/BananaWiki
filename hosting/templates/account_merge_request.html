Merge Request

Merge another hosting account into your own. The source account will be suspended and all its wiki instances will be transferred to your account.

Note: Both accounts must approve the merge. Alternatively, an admin can force the merge.

What Happens During a Merge

  • All wiki instances from the source account are transferred to the target account
  • OAuth links are updated to point to the target account
  • The source account is suspended (can still be accessed by admins)
  • Both accounts must approve before the merge is executed
