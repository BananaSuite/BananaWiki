# Welcome to BananaWiki

BananaWiki keeps your group's pages, categories and history in one place on your own server. Members read and search, editors write, and administrators manage accounts, roles and features.

## How the wiki is organised

Pages are written in Markdown. Categories group pages in the sidebar and can contain subcategories. Your role and your category access decide what you can read and change. Tools such as chat, Kanban boards and canvases appear under **Apps** when an administrator has switched them on.

## First steps

1. Open a page from the sidebar.
2. Type in the field at the top of the sidebar to filter titles, or press Enter to search the text.
3. Click **New page** in the sidebar when a page is missing. A rough first version is fine.
4. If a category you expect is missing, you probably have no access to it. Ask an administrator.

## About this guide

Administrators add this guide from **Administration → Documentation**. They can also download it as Markdown, edit it and bring it back with the bulk Markdown import.
