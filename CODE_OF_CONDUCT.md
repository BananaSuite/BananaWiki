# Code of Conduct

BananaWiki is run by people who also operate it for other people's documentation.
Keep that in mind in issues, pull requests and discussions: someone reading a
thread may be deciding whether to trust this software with their notes.

What is expected:

- Discuss the change, not the person proposing it.
- Say what you actually tested and what you did not. An honest "I only tried
  this on a single wiki, not the hosting platform" is worth more than a
  confident guess.
- Leave other people's data out of reports. Reproduce a bug with content you
  are free to publish.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a feature without owing a long
  justification. Scope is a design decision.

What is not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, sustained disruption,
publishing someone's private information, or posting another operator's
instance data.

A maintainer may edit or hide a comment, lock a thread and block an account
once a discussion has stopped being about the software. Expect that to be
explained rather than silent.

Raise a problem with [Luca Zani (OverloadedTech)](https://github.com/OverloadedTech)
through the contact details on that profile. Vulnerabilities do not belong
here: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) has the private route for those.
